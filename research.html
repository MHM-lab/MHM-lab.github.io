Mental health mapping sits at the intersection of machine learning, neuroscience, psychiatry, and philosophy. Since its founding in 2022, the lab has published in leading venues across these fields. We also hold ourselves to a simple benchmark: for many brain readouts, well-specified regression models perform as well as more complex AI methods, and we report this honestly.
